# Rule 64-4.222 requirements matrix

MMTC STS Tracking System Procedures. Adopted 8-24-26. Transcribed from the adopted
rule document (see `docs/sources.md`). This is the rule that governs what the system
must do every day once it is live.

| ID | Citation | Requirement | Product implication | Enforced by |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| PROC-1 | 64-4.222(2) | A disconnection longer than 2 hours must be reported to the department no later than 3 hours after the disconnection began, by email | Heartbeat monitor, disconnect timer, notice draft with a send-time record | `outage.Outage.notice_status()`, `Workspace.watch()`, `coontie watch`, `coontie outage --send notice` |
| PROC-2 | 64-4.222(2)(a) | During a disconnection, no dispensing unless MMUR status and order/limit are verifiable; if MMUR is unavailable, do not dispense | Hard offline rule in the point of sale; a dispensary module cannot work offline | enforced in `Workspace.record_dispensation()` (refuses, does not warn); MMUR access unverified |
| PROC-3 | 64-4.222(2)(b) | Notify immediately on restoration, and send an itemized CSV of dispensations during the outage within 24 hours | Outage log with the five required columns, plus a 24-hour clock | `Workspace.restore_outage()` → `watch()` drafts both artifacts; `outage.render_dispensations_csv()`, `coontie outage --send report` |
| PROC-4 | 64-4.222(3) + DH5059 | Every DH5059 record reaches the department inside its stated timeframe: 12 hours by default, real time for dispensing, refund, void, and transfers | Per-record sync state and lag measurement | `check_event_timing()` |
| PROC-5 | 64-4.222(4) | Retain all data 3 years; produce native data as CSV within 48 hours of a request | Append-only ledger with a one-command export | `Ledger.export_csv()`, `retention_floor()`, `check_department_csv()` |
| PROC-6 | 64-4.222(5) | Only MMTC people, the internal STS vendor, and the department STS vendor may access the internal STS; third-party access is disciplinable | Role model that excludes any external reader, including analytics products | `check_access()` |
| PROC-7 | 64-4.222(6) | Whole flower dispensed in ounces converts at 1 g = 0.0353 oz | One conversion function used everywhere, never ad hoc math | `grams_to_ounces()`, `ounces_to_grams()` |
| PROC-8 | 64-4.222(7) | A dispensation is recorded under exactly one physician-approved route | Route is a single value per line; validation against the patient's approved routes | `check_dispensation_routes()` |
| PROC-9 | 64-4.222(8) | The department-approved product name is used in the STS and the point of sale | Approved name is an attribute of the product, not a free-text field at sale | `FinalProduct.approved_product_name` |
| PROC-10 | 64-4.222(9) | A unique product ID is assigned to every product type and delivery device and used across systems | ID is mandatory on final product, delivery device, dispensation, and transfer | required fields in `events` |
| PROC-11 | 64-4.222(10) | A transportation manifest is generated from the internal STS for every transport, per s. 381.986(8)(g) | Manifest generation is a system function, not a document someone types | `transport.render_manifest()`, `Workspace.build_manifest()`, `coontie manifest` |
| PROC-12 | 64-4.222(11) | The STS identifies the precise location (room, storage unit, designated area) at all times | Location on every move and every storage-bearing record; no null location states | required `room`/`facility_location` fields |
| PROC-13 | 64-4.222(12) | Non-standard weight or count adjustments need a written justification to the department within 12 hours; processing, dispensation, wholesale, and waste disposal are exempt | Adjustment cause is a required field internally; exempt causes skip the notice | `check_adjustment_justification()`, `amendments.draft_text()`, `coontie amend` |

## Events Coontie adds for this rule

The DH5059 catalog has no event for a disconnection, so Coontie records four of its own in
the same hash-chained ledger. They are Coontie keys, not DH5059 event types:

| Key | When | Why it exists |
|---|---|---|
| `disconnect_start` | The connection is recorded as lost | Starts both clocks; nothing else can be computed without it |
| `disconnect_notice` | The 2-hour threshold passes and the notice is drafted | Proves the notice existed before the 3-hour deadline, independent of when it is sent |
| `disconnect_restored` | The connection is recorded as back | Starts the 24-hour report clock and gives the duration |
| `outage_report` | The restoration notice and the outage CSV are generated | Names both artifacts, so the file the department receives is traceable to a ledger entry |
