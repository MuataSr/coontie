HelsinkiJS"), with a GitHub repository at `github.com/Coonti/Coonti`, preserved in the Wayback Machine.
It is a content management system, dormant, and in a different field from a Florida cannabis compliance
record. It is recorded here as context, not as a licence: a dormant project in another class is not a
blocker for a different good, but the collision is real and a reader deserves to see it named.

## What this means for the project

- The name is usable in the United States on the evidence above, and the risk is small and documented.
- The name is not a trademark claim: this project asserts none, and the `LICENSE` grants copyright
  permission only. A weak common-law association is all that exists, which fits an MIT project.
- Two checks are outstanding and belong to a person with a browser: **Florida Sunbiz**, and any
  commercial register a future sponsor cares about.
- If a sponsor or licensee later wants the name protected, that is a filing decision with a lawyer, and
  nothing here substitutes for it.
