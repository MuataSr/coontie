# Form DH5058: the inventory list

Form **DH5058-OMMU-1/2024**, "Usable Product and Marijuana Delivery Device Inventory
and Unique Product ID". Incorporated in Rule 64-4.221(5)(a), F.A.C.

An MMTC submits this list, together with its written integration plan, to
`ommu_seedtosale@flhealth.gov`. The department requires an `.xlsx` or `.csv` containing
**only** the information below.

Source (fetched 2026-10-21):
`https://knowthefactsmmj.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/64ER24-1-MMTC-Usable-Product-and-Marijuana-Delivery-Device-Inventory-and-Unique-Product-ID.pdf`

## Columns, in the form's order

1. MMTC Name
2. Product Type
3. Route of Administration
4. Marijuana Delivery Device
5. Product Name
6. Product Brand
7. Unique Product ID
8. Tetrahydrocannabinol (THC) Weight
9. Cannabidiol (CBD) Weight
10. Product Weight
11. Product Formulation
12. Cultivar or Strain Name
13. Flavor Name
14. Approval Date
15. Reference Number

## Enumerations, transcribed exactly

**Product Type:** Capsule/Tablet, Concentrate, Vape, Tincture, Topical, Suppository,
Whole Flower, Ground Whole Flower, Gelatin, Chocolate, Lozenge, Pre-rolled marijuana
cigarette, Baked Good, Drink Powder, Marijuana Delivery Device.

**Route of Administration:** Edible, Inhalation, Oral, Sublingual, Suppository, Topical,
Smoking.

**Marijuana Delivery Device:** Yes or No.

Units, from the form's own parentheticals: THC and CBD weights in **milligrams**, product
weight in **grams**, approval date as **yyyy-mm-dd**, reference number exactly as printed
on the product's approval letter, and product formulation as cannabinoids per serving.

## What Coontie does with it

`coontie export dh5058 --out dh5058.csv` writes these 15 columns in this order, with
canonical enumeration values, from the workspace's product catalogue. Loose input from an
incumbent export ("flower", "gummies", "smoke", "rectal") is canonicalised on import, so
the export never carries a value the department will reject.

Two fields Coontie cannot invent: **Approval Date** and **Reference Number**. They come
from the product's approval letter. If the operator has them, include them in the import;
if not, the columns render empty rather than guessed.

## Note on what this list is

It is a product catalogue, not a stock count. There is no quantity column. That surprised
us and it matters for scoping: the integration plan needs the *product types and their
unique IDs*, while quantities live in the daily DH5059 records.
